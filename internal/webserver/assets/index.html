Ports & access

A bind address is evidence, not a claim of internet reachability.

Hostdesk guards traffic addressed to this host through the firewall INPUT path. It does not cover Docker bridge-published ports forwarded through the FORWARD path. Host-network containers use INPUT. Other firewall rules and published proxies remain separate; a public route can provide another path to a service.

Explicit allowlists

Interface access policy

Listed interfaces deny new host-directed inbound TCP/UDP except the allowed ports below. This does not restrict forwarded container traffic. Established connections, loopback, and ICMP remain permitted. Unlisted interfaces stay under the host’s existing rules.

Use exact interface names from this host. Enter individual ports separated by commas, from 1 to 65535; an empty list allows no new ports for that protocol. The broker validates names and protects management ports.
